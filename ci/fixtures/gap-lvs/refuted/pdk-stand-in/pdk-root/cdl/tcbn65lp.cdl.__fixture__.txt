* SYNTHETIC STAND-IN — NOT FOUNDRY CONTENT, and it must never be replaced by any.
*
* This file exists to make one claim falsifiable: "there is no CDL and no GDS
* for the standard-cell, IO or bond-pad libraries anywhere on this system".
* The refuting artefact is a transistor-level netlist over 1 kB under a name the
* probe matches. Its CONTENT is irrelevant to the probe and is invented here:
* the device models are named SYNTH_NMOS / SYNTH_PMOS, which correspond to
* nothing in any process, and the cells are CELL_SYNTHETIC_*, which correspond
* to no library.
*
* IF A REAL CDL EVER ARRIVES ON THIS SITE, DO NOT COPY ANY PART OF IT HERE. This
* is a public repository. The gap closes by `lint` going red against the real
* PDK, not by this fixture growing real content.
*
