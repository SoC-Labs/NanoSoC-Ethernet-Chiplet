## 3. The pad-abstract results (~1,300) — why they are not real geometry

`-output_macros` streams LEF `OBS`/`PIN` regions as drawn metal. A LEF obstruction is a **keep-out
region**, drawn as a solid blanket; real pad metal is slotted. So the abstracts are unmanufacturable
by construction, not marginally out of spec:

| cell | footprint | min dimension | vs `M1_W_3` = 12.0 µm |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PDDW16DGZ_G`, `PVDD2POC_G`, `PVSS2DGZ_G` + 5 more | 25 × 135 µm | 25 µm | 2.08× over, **violates alone** |
| `PFILLER20_G` | 20 × 135 µm | 20 µm | 1.67× over, **violates alone** |
| merged pad ring (left edge) | 135 × 2000 µm | 134–135 µm | 11.2× over |

19 of 51 placements violate in isolation; abutment is an amplifier, not the cause. The left edge
merges to a **single polygon 135 × 2000 µm from 132 placements of 10 masters**.

Stripping the pad abstracts takes all 773 `M1..M7.W.3` results to **0** while leaving every core
result intact. We are **not** submitting that stripped stream — it would also hide the corner
problem in §7, which we would rather you see — but it quantifies the attribution.

Cross-check: the same 117/109 per-layer counts appear under the 2012 deck (DRD 2.1b), so this is
stable across twelve years of rule revisions.

### The controlled experiment

`PO.R.8` is **net-based**: it fires when a gate's extracted net reaches no source/drain anywhere. A
gate behind a macro's input pin has its source/drain in the *driving* cell, outside the macro. In our
stream that driver is a LEF abstract with metal pin shapes and no diffusion at all, so the net has a
gate and nothing else — and Calibre correctly calls it floating, given what it was shown.

Same macro, three contexts, all measured 2026-08-13 with the same deck:

| context | `rf_08k` | `rf_16k` |
|---|---:|---:|
| macro alone as `LAYOUT PRIMARY` (our copy) | 80 | 82 |
| inside **our** chip (surroundings are LEF abstracts) | 80 | 82 |
| macro alone as `LAYOUT PRIMARY` **inside the taped-out GDS** | 80 | 82 |
| inside the **taped-out** chip (surroundings are real Arm cells) | **0** | **0** |

The macro is byte-equivalent in both files — same geometry, same 33 layer/datatype pairs. Only the
surroundings differ. A previously taped-out design carrying these macros reports **zero** from them.

**Running a macro standalone cannot discriminate**, because standalone *is* the black-box condition.
An earlier version of this analysis ran exactly that test, got a matching number, and concluded the
violations were inherent. It compared two contexts that share the defect. The taped-out chip is the
control that was missing.

**Scope of proof.** `rf_08k` and `rf_16k` (162 of the 691) are demonstrated directly — both exist in
the reference design. The remaining 529 come from macro types absent there (`rf_01k`, `rf_32k`,
`flash_cache_*`, `eth_rom_via`, and a 64-wordline ROM against the reference's 32) and rest on the
same mechanism rather than their own control.

**We tested whether this is a rule-revision artefact. It is not:**

| deck | DRD version | `PO.R.8` |
|---|---|---|
| `CLN65S_9M_6X1Z1U.26_2a` | VER 2.6_2 (2024) | **691** |
| `CLN65S_9M_6X1Z1U.21b` | VER 2.1 (2012) | **691** |

Identical. The memories have carried these since at least 2012.

**TSMC's own SRAM waiver mechanism is present and active, but does not reach this rule.** The vendor
ships `SRAMDMY` markers on three datatypes of a dedicated marker layer and they survive into our
stream; the deck maps all three datatypes and the deck's SRAM-exclude layer filters ~30 derived
layers. But the floating-gate chain is built from the **raw** poly and diffusion layers, so the
filter never applies. The markers also sit on bitcell-array cells, whereas these results are in
control logic and ROM cells.

The vendor's own release note (ARM `rf_sp_hdf_hvt_rvt` r0p0, §5 *Known issues & work-around*) waives
`M5–M9.DN.1L` density and four `PATHCHK` ERC categories in these macros — but **not** `PO.R.8`.

Its §7 also records the conditions the IP was verified under, which differ from ours:

| switch | ARM's qualification | ours |
|---|---|---|
| `BEOL_STACK` | `9M_6X2Z` | `9M_6X1Z1U` |
| `WLCSP_SEALRING` | `FALSE` | ON in the shipped deck (see Q3 in doc 27) |
| DRC command file | `T-N65-CL-DR-001-C1` Ver 2.0b (2010) | Ver 2.6_2a (2024) |

We match ARM on the other 13 of 15 applicable switches, including `FULL_CHIP TRUE` — so `PO.R.8` was
in scope for their qualification.

**And the one `PO.R.8` waiver Arm does publish says the same thing we do.** It is not for the memory
compilers at all — it is for the `sc12` standard-cell library, and its stated justification is that
the rule is waived *at IP level because it must be checked at chip level*. That is the mechanism in
§4's controlled experiment, arriving from the vendor's side: `PO.R.8` is not answerable about a cell
in isolation, so an IP-level waiver is the correct disposition for it and a chip-level check is where
the real answer comes from. Searching for a memory-compiler `PO.R.8` waiver therefore fails for a
reason, and finding one would not have helped.

**Ask — REVISED, and this replaces the waiver request.** We are **not** asking for a `PO.R.8` waiver
and these should not be routed to Arm as a vendor defect. What we need is one confirmation:

> After your import of the standard-cell and IO back-end layout, does `PO.R.8` report **zero** from
> the compiled memories on this design?

We predict zero, on the evidence in §4. We cannot measure it here — the prediction is falsifiable
only on your side, because this site holds no cell layout to import. **Please report the number you
get either way**, including if it is 691: that would falsify the analysis above and it should be

## 5. Antenna — 1,549 results, all on one net

Every classic gate-antenna check is **empty**: `A.R.1`/`A.R.2` POLY, `A.R.3` CO, `A.R.4`/`A.R.7`
VIA1–8, `A.R.6`/`A.R.8` M1–M9, `A.R.10`–`A.R.13`. 156 of 204 report files are header-only.

All 1,549 results are `A.R.8.3`/`.1`/`.2` — a plasma-charging **floating-metal spacing** rule, not a
gate-oxide ratio. It triggers on an absolute *accumulated area* of metal that **does not connect to
OD**, sited within a short spacing window of metal that does; the three sub-rules are three
successively larger area tiers. What matters here is the shape of the rule, not its constants: a
power mesh that Calibre believes is floating will clear the smallest tier easily.

> Foundry `A.R.8` accumulated-area thresholds and spacing window redacted — TSMC licence forbids
> reproduction. Source: the `A.R.8` rules in `MAIN_DRC_TopMu/CLN65S_9M_6X1Z1U.26_2a`. Read them
> there before judging any antenna result.

**All 1,549 are one net** (`NET=3`), whose largest M7 polygon is exactly **1600.000 × 2000.000 µm —
the die outline** — totalling 901,003 µm², 28% of die area. That is the power mesh.

Innovus `check_process_antenna` reports **No Violations Found** on this and all eleven other routed
runs, and it is the only tool with access to the standard-cell gate areas (2,865 `ANTENNAGATEAREA`

routing (16 → 0).

**Status: unresolved on our side.** Either the missing standard-cell diffusion makes our power grid
look floating to Calibre (artefact, resolves at your import), or the grid genuinely does not reach
the macro power pins (a real defect we must fix). We are testing this now and will report before
submission. We flag it rather than omit it.

## 6. Latch-up: `LUP.* = 0` is not a clean result

Pads are recognised by `CB` (passivation opening). **`CB` does not appear anywhere in our
stream**, because it is absent from `tpbn65v`'s LEF and `-output_macros` can only emit what the LEF
contains. The documented alternative for abstract-only bondpads, `#DEFINE DEFINE_PAD_BY_TEXT`,
requires top-cell pin text on the foundry's pin-text layer range; our top cell carries **48 labels,
all on a single M3 text layer, all signal names, none power**. Enabling it against that empty input produced **222,350 false latch-up
results**, so it is currently off.

**Therefore no pad is recognised, and the latch-up and ESD checks are silently disarmed.** We are
adding the required pin text so the checks can be armed; until then, treat `LUP.* = 0` as
*not checked*. The same gap disables the `AP.W.2` "not inside UBM/CB/CB2" bond-pad exemption.

## 8. Method notes (so the numbers can be checked)

- Totals are taken from the summary's authoritative `(BY CELL)` and flat statistics sections, which
  agree. Naïve `grep RULECHECK` **double-counts** (the file states every count twice, once per
  section) — an early triage here reported 7,527 for what is 2,509.
- `CN` (cell-name) records in the ASCII results database are **sticky**: Calibre omits the field when
  it would repeat the previous result's. There are 262 `CN` lines for 2,508 results, so grepping
  `^CN` undercounts attribution roughly tenfold.
- **Saturation** cannot be detected by comparing the count and origcount fields — Calibre writes the
  truncated value into both. The only valid test is whether any rulecheck equals
  `DRC MAXIMUM RESULTS` exactly. All numbers quoted here pass that test (0 capped; max single
  rulecheck 691).
- `check_connectivity` output in our logs stops at `Number of errors exceeds the limit 1000`, so the
  open-net counts in those reports are truncated and are not quoted here.

### A.3 Latch-up remains NOT-CHECKED, and labels did not change that

`DEFINE_PAD_BY_TEXT` reads text on the foundry's pin-text layer range, which the
`LEFPIN` rows now populate. It still cannot fire, for a reason upstream of the text:

Tracing the deck (do not reproduce foundry rule text here — read
`MAIN_DRC_TopMu/CLN65S_9M_6X1Z1U.26_2a` directly, and see §5 of the deck's
latch-up section): the post-driver active layer is derived by intersecting
**source/drain diffusion** with the text-identified pad metal, and every one of
the five expressions that consume a `*_BY_TEXT` layer takes diffusion or n-well
as its other operand.

Every path therefore terminates in diffusion. The IO cells in this stream carry
shapes on M1–M7 only — metal only, no OD, CO, PP or NP. With no
diffusion the chain is empty and every latch-up rule downstream reports zero
whether the define is on or off. That is NOT-CHECKED, not clean. Unblocking it
needs the IO/pad **layout**, which is the §27 broker question.

`CHECK_FLOATING_GATE_BY_TEXT` does not exist in `CLN65S_9M_6X1Z1U.26_2a`, and
neither does `PO.R.19`. This deck has `PO.R.1/2/3/4/6/8` only. Guidance citing
those names is for a deck revision this site does not have — worth raising with
the broker directly.
